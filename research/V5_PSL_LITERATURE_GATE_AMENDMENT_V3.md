# V5-PSL literature-gate v3 access amendment

Date: 2026-08-30

## Decision timing

This amendment was adopted at pre-amendment commit
`4668c3e8f5432adb0b82e04eb0490ba55be48e05`. At that point:

- no V5-PSL public-pressure run or audit existed;
- no V5-PSL candidate lock existed;
- no third-party PUBLIC or SECRET archive had been opened;
- no V5-PSL promotion result existed.

The amendment is based only on lawful full-text availability, not experimental
outcomes.

## Original and amended gates

The v2 gate required full-text reviews for all 13 registered sources. Eleven were
obtained and reviewed. The following direct neighbors remained closed access after
publisher, Crossref, OpenAlex, and user searches:

- Wang and Zhao 2021, `10.1109/APR52552.2021.00012`;
- Hofer and Wotawa 2013, `10.1109/ISSREW.2013.6688892`.

The v3 gate requires full-text reviews for the 11 obtainable sources and explicit
availability disclosures for the two closed sources. All seven claim areas still
have at least one required full-text primary source. The two disclosed sources remain
in the claim matrix and receipt, but they cannot support novelty or superiority
claims.

## Conservative claim rule

The available publisher evidence is sufficient only to preserve broad prior-art
constraints:

- domain invariants for spreadsheet detection or repair cannot be claimed as new;
- candidate mutation, genetic-programming spreadsheet debugging, and automatic
  spreadsheet repair cannot be claimed as new.

No implementation-level comparison with either unavailable source is permitted.
If a legal full text later becomes available, it must be reviewed and the claim
matrix updated before using any finer distinction.

## Reporting rule

Reports must say `11 required full texts verified plus 2 closed-access disclosures`.
They must not say `13/13 full texts verified`, omit the two sources, or treat lack of
access as evidence of originality.
